Design notes
------------
- Palette: deep slate, muted blue, soft gray, and a restrained green accent.
- All technology labels are drawn in one SVG with identical tile dimensions and text baselines.
- The banner and footer are static vector artwork.
- terminal.gif is the only animated asset; only the terminal cursor blinks.
- No generated portraits, anime artwork, external badge grids, statistics widgets, or third-party icon packs.
